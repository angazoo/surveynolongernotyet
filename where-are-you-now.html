Where are you now?

For anyone standing in the no longer / not yet.

This is for people trying to bring something into the world — a project, a product, a body of work, a practice, a venture, a vision — who've landed in the same strange place: the old way of working has stopped fitting, but the new thing hasn't taken shape yet. Some are there by choice. Many are there because the ground moved under them — the world is shifting fast, and it's reaching into people's work whether they asked it to or not.

The tricky part is that this territory is never just one problem. Sometimes you know exactly what you want to do, and the struggle is in the doing. Sometimes you feel ready to move but have no idea toward what. Sometimes you find a direction and then the whole thing shifts halfway through, and you're reoriented before you've begun. And usually it's not one of these — it's several at once, tangled together. Finding, choosing, daring, doing, and adapting all bleed into each other.

So this isn't a quiz that sorts you into a box. It's more ecological than that — a way of looking honestly at where you actually are right now, across a few dimensions, and seeing how they interact. It takes about 10–12 minutes. There are no right answers, "I don't know yet" is a real answer to several of these, and wherever it says so, pick as many as fit.

This is fully anonymous unless you choose otherwise. Your answers carry no name, no email, nothing that identifies you. At the very end you can — if you want to — leave a way to reach you. Entirely up to you, and nothing here depends on it.
